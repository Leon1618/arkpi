# Eye Injuries

## Chemical in the eye: emergency

1. **Call 000** (112 from a mobile).
2. **Rinse the eye with cool water for 20 minutes.** Tilt the head back and
   **towards the injured side**, and hold the eye open with your fingers.
3. **Don't let the chemical run into the other eye.**
4. Take out contact lenses, unless the surface of the eye is badly damaged.

## Something stuck in the eye: emergency

**Don't try to remove it.** Go to the nearest emergency department or call
**000**. Same for any cut, or anything that has **pierced** the eye.

## Speck or grit on the surface

1. Look up and pull the **lower** eyelid down. If you can see it, lift it off
   with the corner of a **moist cloth**.
2. Can't see it? Pull the **upper** eyelid up and look again.
3. Still there? **Rinse gently** with clean water or sterile saline.
4. Still there after that? **Cover the eye** and get medical help.

## A blow to the eye

Hold something cold against it to reduce swelling, like a bag of frozen peas
wrapped in a tea towel. **See a doctor urgently** if the pain gets worse, your
vision changes, or you see black spots.

## Don't

- Don't rub the eye, even if it hurts or itches.
- Don't put drops, ointment or any medicine in the eye.
- Don't try to remove anything stuck in the eye.
- Don't touch your eyes with dirty hands.
