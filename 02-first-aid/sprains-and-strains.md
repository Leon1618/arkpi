# Sprains and Strains

A sprain is a stretched or torn ligament (often an ankle or wrist). A strain is
a stretched or torn muscle. If you can't tell whether a bone is broken, treat it
as broken (see [broken-bones.md](broken-bones.md)).

## RICE, for the first 1 to 3 days

- **Rest** the injured part. Don't put weight on an injured leg.
- **Ice:** a cold pack or ice wrapped in cloth for **15 to 20 minutes at a
  time**, several times a day. **Never put ice directly on the skin.**
- **Compression:** wrap a bandage around it to limit swelling.
- **Elevation:** raise it **above the level of the heart** while resting.

Use RICE only for the first **24 to 72 hours**. After that, gentle movement and
putting weight on it, as much as is comfortable, helps healing.

For pain, paracetamol can help. Anti-inflammatories (like ibuprofen) should be
used with caution. Ask a pharmacist or doctor if you can.

## Get urgent medical help if

- They **can't put weight on it or walk**
- There's **extreme pain, swelling or the limb looks deformed**
- You're **not sure if it's broken or dislocated**
- There's **chest pain**, or pain that doesn't seem to come from a sprain

See a doctor if it isn't getting better after a few days. Health advice from a
nurse, 24/7: **healthdirect 1800 022 222**.
