# Dehydration and Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS)

Diarrhea, vomiting, heat and too little clean water can all cause dehydration.
**Severe dehydration is life-threatening**, especially for babies and young
children.

## Signs

**Mild to moderate:**

- Thirst, dry or sticky mouth
- Peeing less, darker yellow urine
- Dry, cool skin
- Headache, muscle cramps

**Severe (get medical help):**

- Little or no urine, or very dark amber urine
- Dry, shriveled skin; sunken eyes
- Dizziness, fast heartbeat and breathing
- Irritability, confusion, listlessness
- Shock or passing out

## Oral rehydration solution (ORS)

ORS replaces the water **and salts** the body loses. Ready-made ORS packets are
sold in pharmacies. Mix them with clean, treated water (see
[../03-water/making-water-safe.md](../03-water/making-water-safe.md)).

**No packets? Make it at home (WHO recipe):**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Safe drinking water | **1 liter** |
| Sugar | **6 teaspoons** |
| Salt | **½ teaspoon** |

Measure carefully, with flat, level spoons and an accurately measured liter.
Too much salt or sugar makes it harmful, and too little makes it weak.

**How much to give each day (CDC):**

| Who | ORS per day |
|---|---|
| Babies and toddlers with diarrhea | **½ liter** |
| Children with vomiting or diarrhea | **1 liter** |
| Adults with vomiting or diarrhea | **3 liters** |

- **Start as soon as symptoms begin**, and keep them drinking even if they
  vomit some of it.
- **Babies:** keep breastfeeding or giving formula as well.
- Go to a clinic as soon as you can, and **keep giving ORS on the way**.
- No ORS and no ingredients? Give safe water, broth or other fluids. **Avoid
  juice, soft drinks and sports drinks** for diarrhea; they can make it worse.

Illustrated steps: [rehydration-poster-cdc.pdf](rehydration-poster-cdc.pdf)
(CDC, in this folder).

## Get medical help right away if

- They can't drink enough to keep up, or have severe diarrhea
- They are **confused, drowsy or dizzy**, have a seizure or pass out
- Fever is over **102 °F (38.9 °C)**
- They don't improve, or get worse

## Don't

- **Don't take salt tablets.** They can cause serious problems.
