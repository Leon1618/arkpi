# Heat Illness (Heat Exhaustion and Heat Stroke)

Heat illness gets worse in stages. **Heat stroke can cause brain damage,
organ failure and death.**

## Signs

| Stage | Signs |
|---|---|
| **Heat cramps** | Muscle cramps (legs, belly), tiredness, thirst, heavy sweating |
| **Heat exhaustion** | Cool, moist skin; sweating; strong thirst; dark urine; dizziness; headache; nausea or vomiting; weakness; fast, shallow breathing; fast, weak pulse |
| **Heat stroke** (emergency) | All of the above **plus** temperature over **104 °F (40 °C)**, confusion, strange behavior, slurred speech, seizures, extreme drowsiness or passing out. **Sweating may stop.** |

## What to do

1. **Lay them down somewhere cool** and raise their feet about **12 inches
   (30 cm)**.
2. **Cool them down:** cool, wet cloths or water on the skin, a fan, and cold
   packs on the **neck, groin and armpits**.
3. **If they're alert**, give a sports drink or slightly salted drink, in
   **small sips: about half a cup every 15 minutes**. Cool water if nothing
   else is available.
4. **Cramps:** give fluids and firmly massage the cramping muscle until it
   relaxes.

## Get emergency help if

- They **pass out**, at any time
- They become **confused** or have a **seizure**
- Fever is over **102 °F (38.9 °C)**
- They have a fast pulse or fast breathing (signs of heat stroke)
- They show signs of shock (see [../01-emergency/shock.md](../01-emergency/shock.md))
- They don't improve, or get worse

## Don't

- **Don't give fever medicine** (aspirin, acetaminophen/paracetamol). It won't
  help and may harm.
- **Don't give salt tablets.**
- Don't give alcohol or caffeine.
- Don't rub alcohol on the skin.
- **Don't give anything by mouth** if they are vomiting or unconscious.
