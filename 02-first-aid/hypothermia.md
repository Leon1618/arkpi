# Hypothermia (Body Too Cold)

Hypothermia happens when the body loses heat faster than it can make it.
**It can kill.** Get help early.

## Signs

- **Shivering that's hard to control** (in extreme cold, shivering may stop)
- Confusion, drowsiness
- Pale, cold skin
- Weakness, clumsiness
- Slow breathing or heart rate

## What to do

1. **Call emergency services right away**, especially if they are confused.
2. **If they're unconscious**, check breathing. Start CPR if needed (see
   [../01-emergency/cpr.md](../01-emergency/cpr.md)). Give rescue breaths if
   they take **fewer than 6 breaths per minute**.
3. **Get them out of the cold.** Indoors, at room temperature, under warm
   blankets. If you can't go indoors: shield them from wind, put something
   between them and the cold ground, and cover their **head and neck**.
4. **Severe hypothermia:** move them **gently** and with as little effort on
   their part as possible.
5. **Replace wet or tight clothing** with dry, loose clothing.
6. **Warm the core:** warm compresses on the **neck, chest and groin**.
7. If they are **alert and can swallow easily**, give warm, sweet drinks with
   no alcohol.
8. **Stay with them** until help arrives.

## Don't

- **Don't assume someone found still and cold is dead.** Start help anyway.
- **Don't use direct heat** (hot water, a heating pad, a heat lamp).
- **Don't give alcohol.**
