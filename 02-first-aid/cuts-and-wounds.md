# Cuts and Puncture Wounds

For heavy bleeding that won't stop, go to
[../01-emergency/severe-bleeding.md](../01-emergency/severe-bleeding.md) first.

## Minor cut

1. **Wash your hands** with soap.
2. **Wash the cut well** with mild soap and water. Always wash it, even if it
   looks clean.
3. **Press on it** to stop the bleeding.
4. Put on **antibacterial ointment** and cover it with a clean bandage that
   won't stick to the wound.

## Minor puncture (nail, splinter, thorn)

1. **Wash your hands** with soap.
2. **Rinse the wound under running water for 5 minutes**, then wash it with
   soap.
3. **Look into it without poking around.**
   - If something is stuck in it, **leave it in place** and get medical help.
   - If part of the object seems to be missing (a broken tip, for example), get
     medical help.
4. Put on antibacterial ointment and cover it with a clean, non-stick bandage.

## Get medical help soon if

- The wound is **deeper than ¼ inch (about 6 mm)**, large, on the face, or
  reaches the bone. It may need stitches.
- It's a **human or animal bite**.
- It was caused by a **fishhook, a rusty object, or stepping on a nail**.
- Something is stuck in the wound.
- The person hasn't had a **tetanus shot in the last 10 years**.
- It shows **signs of infection**:
  - warmth and redness around it
  - pain or throbbing
  - fever
  - swelling
  - a **red streak** spreading from the wound
  - pus

## Get emergency help if

- Bleeding is severe or doesn't stop after **10 minutes of pressure**
- The person can't feel or move the injured area normally
- They have other serious injuries

## Don't

- Don't breathe on an open wound.
- Don't try to clean a **major** wound, especially once bleeding is under
  control.
- **Don't pull out a long or deeply stuck object.**
- Don't push or pick dirt out of the wound.
- Don't push body parts that are sticking out back in. Cover them with clean
  material until help arrives.
