# Arkpi: Offline Knowledge Ark

Survival guides and essential information that work **without internet**.
Copy this folder to a computer, USB drive or phone, and everything is there.

> **In an emergency right now:**
> [Severe bleeding](01-emergency/severe-bleeding.md) ·
> [No safe water](03-water/making-water-safe.md)
>
> If phones work, call your local emergency number first
> (911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 190/192/193 in Brazil).

browser, print it, or paste it into a message.

## Sharing it

- **Copy the whole folder.** That's the whole backup: no installation needed.
- **Leave out `private/`** if you made one (see below). It's for your own
  family information and is never uploaded to GitHub.

## Rules for content

1. **Medical and safety guidance comes only from trusted sources**: CDC,
   FEMA / Ready.gov, DHS, WHO, Red Cross, public-domain military manuals.
   Never from memory.
2. **Each page ends with a Sources section** listing where the information came
   from and the date it was checked.
3. **One topic per page**, named in plain words, inside the right numbered
   folder. Files it needs (images, PDFs) go next to it.
4. These pages don't replace training. Take a first-aid course while you can.

US federal government publications (CDC, FEMA, Ready.gov, DHS) are in the
public domain. Content from other organizations is summarized and linked
rather than copied, unless its license allows sharing.

## Your private information

Create a `private/` folder for family contacts, meeting points, medical
information and copies of documents. It is ignored by git, so it never ends
up on GitHub. Remember to leave it out when sharing the folder.
