# Offline Map Apps

A phone's GPS works **without mobile signal or internet**: it listens to
satellites. What you need is a map stored on the phone **before** the outage.

## Organic Maps (free)

- Free, open source, no ads and no tracking.
- Uses OpenStreetMap, the same data as the maps in this folder.
- Every feature works offline: search, turn-by-turn directions for walking,
  cycling and driving, hiking trails, contour lines and elevation.
- For iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play, F-Droid).

**Set it up now, while you have internet:**

1. Install **Organic Maps**.
2. Download the map for your region (for Noosa: Queensland). Maps of other
   places you might evacuate to are worth downloading too.
3. Turn on flight mode and check that you can still search for an address and
   get directions.
4. Update the maps every few months.

## Save these places in the app

Mark them as bookmarks so you can find them in the dark or in smoke:

- Your home and your family meeting point
- The hospitals, fire, police and ambulance stations from
  [key-places-noosa.md](key-places-noosa.md)
- Where you would evacuate to (family or friends out of the flood or bushfire
  area)

## Saving battery

The screen uses the most power. Turn the brightness down, use flight mode (GPS
keeps working), and only open the map when you need it.
