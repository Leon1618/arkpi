# 10 · Navigation and Maps

Finding your way without GPS or online maps.

## Pages

- [Maps of Noosa](maps-noosa.md): printable maps with emergency services marked
- [Key Places: Noosa Shire](key-places-noosa.md): hospitals, police, fire, ambulance, pharmacies, fuel, water, with GPS coordinates
- [Offline Map Apps](offline-map-apps.md): maps on your phone with no signal
- [Finding Direction Without GPS](finding-direction.md): sun, watch, Southern Cross, moon

## Update the maps

`tools/make-map.py` redraws the maps and the list of places from the latest
OpenStreetMap data (needs internet). Edit the areas at the top of the script to
map another region.
