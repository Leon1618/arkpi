# Generators and Backup Power

> **Generators kill through carbon monoxide.** Run one **only outdoors**, at
> least **6 m (20 feet)** from windows, doors and attached garages. Never in the
> house, garage, carport or under the house. See
> [../01-emergency/carbon-monoxide.md](../01-emergency/carbon-monoxide.md).

## Using a portable generator

- **Outdoors only**, away from windows, doors and vents.
- **Keep it dry.** Touching a wet generator, or anything plugged into it, can
  give you an electric shock.
- Plug appliances in with **heavy-duty extension cords**.
- **Let it cool down before refuelling.**
- Have a working **carbon monoxide alarm on every level** of the home.

## Before the outage

- If someone relies on **powered medical equipment** or **refrigerated
  medicine**, ask the doctor now for a backup plan (see
  [../02-first-aid/medicines-in-an-emergency.md](../02-first-aid/medicines-in-an-emergency.md)).
- **NBN phone and internet won't work in a power cut**, even with a backup at
  home. Keep a charged mobile, a power bank and a battery or wind-up radio.
- Keep torches and spare batteries (see
  [../06-fire-and-light/safe-lighting.md](../06-fire-and-light/safe-lighting.md)).

## Car batteries

A car can charge phones from its USB or 12 V socket. **Only run the engine
outdoors**, never in a garage. Car batteries can give off **explosive hydrogen
gas**, so keep sparks and flames away from them.

## Report problems

- **Fallen power lines:** keep well away, treat them as live, and call
  **Energex 13 19 62** (or 000 if someone is in danger).
- Power faults: **Energex 13 62 62**.
