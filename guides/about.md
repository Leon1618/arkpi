# About & Sources

Arkpi is a personal offline library for use when the internet is down.

## Rules for content

1. **Medical and safety guidance comes only from vetted sources**: CDC, FEMA /
   Ready.gov, DHS, WHO, Red Cross, and public-domain military field manuals.
2. Every guide records its `sources` (with the date retrieved) and the date it
   was last `reviewed` in its front matter.
3. When a source updates its advice, the guide is updated and the dates change.
4. These guides are not a replacement for training. Take a first-aid course
   (Red Cross, Stop the Bleed) while you still can.

## Licensing

US federal government publications (CDC, FEMA, Ready.gov, DHS) are in the
public domain. Content from other organizations is summarized and linked
rather than copied, unless its license allows redistribution.
