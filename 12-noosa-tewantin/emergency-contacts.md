# Emergency Contacts: Noosa / Tewantin (QLD)

> **Life-threatening emergency: 000** (Police, Fire, Ambulance)
> From a mobile you can also dial **112**.
> On the water: **VHF channel 16** (Marine Rescue Queensland).

Print this page and keep it with your emergency kit. Phones and internet may be
down: **NBN home phones and internet need power at both ends**, so they can
stop working in a power outage even if you have a backup at home.

## Online (when the internet works)

- **Noosa Disaster Dashboard**, the council's main source during a disaster:
  https://disaster.noosa.qld.gov.au (road conditions, power outages, warnings,
  river heights, evacuation centres, flood cameras)
- **Noosa ALERT**: free SMS and email warnings. Sign up on the dashboard.
- Bureau of Meteorology: https://www.bom.gov.au
- Queensland alerts: https://www.qld.gov.au/alerts
- Get Ready Queensland: https://www.getready.qld.gov.au
