# Bushfire (Noosa)

You don't have to live in the bush to be at risk: being close enough for
**embers, burning material and smoke** to reach you is enough. Fires can move
**faster than you can run** and change direction suddenly.

Fire season in South-East Queensland usually starts in **July/August** and can
run into summer. Check the daily **Fire Danger Rating** (see
[warnings.md](warnings.md)).

## Decide now: leave early, or stay and defend?

If you plan to leave, go **well before** a fire threatens, while the roads are
still safe.

Only plan to stay if your property is well prepared, you are physically and
mentally ready, and you have the equipment and know how to use it. On a
**Catastrophic** day, leave bushfire risk areas.

## Before a fire

- Write a **Bushfire Survival Plan** with your household.
- Keep grass short and vegetation cleared around the house.
- Move flammable things away from the house: woodpiles, boxes, hanging
  baskets, garden furniture, **gas bottles**.
- Keep driveways clear for fire trucks.
- Don't dump garden waste in reserves or bushland.
- Prepare your emergency and evacuation kits.
- **When a warning tells you to act, act immediately.** Leave when advised,
  even if it feels safe where you are, and go in the direction advised.

## If you are sheltering as the fire front arrives

- Bring hoses and fittings inside. Go inside.
- Shelter on the **side of the house away from the fire**.
- **Drink lots of water.**
- Patrol for embers inside, especially in the **roof space**.
- Check on family and pets. Keep a way out.
- Close windows and doors, take down curtains, and move furniture away from
  windows.

## After the fire front passes

- **Emergency: 000** (or 112 from a mobile).
- Listen to the radio for Queensland Fire Department warnings.
- If you evacuated, wait for the all-clear before leaving your safe place.
- Use a mask or a damp cloth over your face against smoke.
- Check the property for **live electricity, leaking gas, sewage leaks, hot
  embers**, hanging branches and structural damage. If unsure, ask emergency
  services. Don't take risks.
- Keep drinking water.
