# Heatwave (Noosa)

A heatwave is **three or more days** of maximum and minimum temperatures that
are unusual for the area. **Severe heatwaves have killed more people than any
other natural hazard in Australia.** They can also make power and transport
fail.

Most at risk: the elderly, pregnant women, babies and young children, but
anyone can be affected.

## Before

- Check the Noosa Disaster Dashboard and the Bureau of Meteorology.
- Plan to be indoors or somewhere air-conditioned.
- Fit blinds or curtains to keep heat out. Check fans and air-conditioners
  work.
- Have enough food, water and medicine.

## During

- **Stay out of the sun, especially 11 am to 3 pm.**
- Wear loose, cool clothes; hat, sunglasses and sunscreen outside.
- Take cool showers or splash yourself with water.
- **Drink plenty of water.** Avoid caffeine, alcohol and heavy protein meals.
- Close curtains and blinds. Avoid physical effort.

**Know the warning signs:** breathlessness, chest pain, confusion, intense
thirst, weakness, dizziness, cramps that won't stop. See
[../02-first-aid/heat-illness.md](../02-first-aid/heat-illness.md). For a
life-threatening emergency, call **000**.

## After

Check on friends, neighbours and anyone less able to look after themselves.
