# Local Hazards: Noosa Shire

**Cyclones and flooding are the most common disasters here.** Noosa can also
have bushfire, severe storms, storm tide, heatwave, tsunami, earthquake and
landslide.

**Main disaster season: November to April.** Noosa Council suggests setting
aside a weekend each **October** to get your home and family ready.

## What has happened here

| Hazard | Local history |
|---|---|
| **Cyclone** | 48 tropical or ex-tropical cyclones have affected Noosa since 1887. **Cyclone Daisy** crossed directly over Noosa in February 1972 (Category 2). |
| **Flood** | The **Noosa River** catchment (most of the shire) and the **Mary River** catchment flood. Last major flood: **1992**, about 540 lots flooded. In April 2009 a flash flood in the **Kin Kin Creek** catchment brought about 900 mm of rain in under 6 hours. |
| **Storm tide** | Minor storm tides are frequent (some "king tides" are actually minor storm tides). The most recent notable one was during **Cyclone Oma** (February 2019). |
| **East Coast Lows** | Intense storms off the coast, about 10 significant ones a year, at any time of year: damaging winds and surf, heavy rain, erosion, flooding. |
| **Bushfire** | The **2019 Peregian, Cooroibah and North Shore fires** burned more than 2,000 hectares. |
| **Tornado** | A tornado hit **Kin Kin** in 1971, killing three people. Kin Kin had tornadoes again as recently as 1984. |
| **Landslide** | Black Mountain. |

## Is my home at risk?

Check these **now**, while the internet works:

- **Flood maps:** Noosa Council has minor/moderate and major/extreme flood maps
  for 27 localities, including Tewantin, Noosaville and Noosa Heads. Run
  `./tools/download-local.sh` to save the Tewantin-area maps into
  `12-noosa-tewantin/downloads/`, or see
  https://www.noosa.qld.gov.au/community/maps/disaster-mapping
- **Storm tide evacuation zone:** check your address on the Disaster Dashboard
  (https://disaster.noosa.qld.gov.au). If your home is in a zone, plan to
  evacuate. If it was **built before 1982**, or you're not sure, ask the
  council about its construction ((07) 5329 6500).
- **Bushfire prone areas:** interactive map on the council's disaster mapping
  page (link above).
