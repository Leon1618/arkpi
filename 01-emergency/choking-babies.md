# Choking: Babies Under 1 Year

For adults and children over 1, see [choking.md](choking.md).

**Call 000** (112 from a mobile) **straight away.** Stay on the phone and follow
their instructions.

## Steps

1. **Lay the baby face down along your forearm**, head lower than the body.
   Support the head and shoulders with your hand and hold the mouth open with
   your fingers.
2. **Up to 5 back blows:** sharp blows between the shoulder blades with the heel
   of your hand. **Check after each blow.** If you can see the object, gently
   remove it with your little finger.
3. **Still blocked? Up to 5 chest thrusts:** turn the baby face up on a firm
   surface. **2 fingers on the lower half of the breastbone.** Check after each
   thrust.
4. **Keep alternating** 5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts until it clears or help
   arrives.
5. **Baby goes limp or unconscious: start CPR, compressions first** (see
   [cpr-babies-and-children.md](cpr-babies-and-children.md)).
