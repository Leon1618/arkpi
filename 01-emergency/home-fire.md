# Fire in the Home

## Get out

1. **Get low and crawl** under the smoke to your exit. Smoke and poisonous
   gases collect near the ceiling first.
2. **Before opening a door, feel the doorknob and the door.** If either is hot,
   or smoke is coming around it, **keep it closed** and use another way out.
3. If you do open a door, **open it slowly**, ready to shut it fast if there's
   heavy smoke or fire.
4. Once out, **stay out**, and call emergency services (911 in the US).
5. If you can't reach someone who needs help, get out and tell the
   firefighters. Same for trapped pets.

## If you're trapped

1. **Close the door.** Block vents and gaps around doors with cloth or tape to
   keep smoke out.
2. **Call emergency services** and say exactly where you are.
3. **Signal at a window** with a light-colored cloth or a flashlight.

## If your clothes catch fire

1. **Stop, drop** to the ground, and **cover your face** with your hands.
2. **Roll** over and over until the fire is out. If you can't roll, smother
   the flames with a blanket or towel.
3. **Cool the burn** with water for 3 to 5 minutes, cover it with a clean, dry
   cloth, and get medical help. See [burns.md](burns.md).

## After the fire

- **Don't go back in** until the fire department says it's safe.
- **Don't reconnect utilities** (gas, electricity) yourself.
- Contact a disaster relief service (such as the Red Cross) for temporary
  housing, food and medicine.
- For insurance: list damaged property and keep damaged items until the list is
  done, save receipts for fire-related costs, and tell your mortgage company.
