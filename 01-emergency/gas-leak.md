# Gas Leak

## Signs

- **Smell:** natural gas has an added **"rotten egg"** smell. Near pipelines
  you may also smell petroleum or another unusual odor.
- **Sound:** an unusual **hissing or roaring**.
- **Sight (outdoors, near pipelines):** dead or discolored plants, pooled
  liquid, or a cloud of vapor or mist.

## Do

1. **Make sure gas appliances are turned all the way OFF** (only on your way
   out; don't search for the leak).
2. **Leave the area on foot**, right away.
3. **Call emergency services (911 in the US) from a neighbor's house or
   somewhere well away from the leak.** Then call your gas company if you know
   the number.
4. **Explain the situation**: your name, phone number, what happened and where.
5. **Warn others**, only if it's safe, to stay away and not create sparks.

## Don't

Anything that makes a spark can ignite the gas:

- **Don't turn any light switches, garage door openers or other electrical
  switches on or off.**
- **Don't use a phone or cell phone near the leak.**
- **Don't light matches or create any flame.**
- **Don't start an engine of any kind**, and don't drive into a gas or vapor
  cloud.
- Don't touch or breathe in leaking liquids.
