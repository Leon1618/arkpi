# Flood

> **Turn Around, Don't Drown!**
> **6 inches (15 cm)** of moving water can knock you down.
> **1 foot (30 cm)** of moving water can sweep a car away.

## During a flood

- **If told to evacuate, go immediately.**
- **Don't walk, swim or drive through floodwater.**
- Don't drive around barricades. They are there to keep you out of flooded
  areas.
- Stay off bridges over fast-moving water. They can wash away without warning.

**If you're trapped:**

- **In a car in fast-moving water:** stay inside. If water rises inside the
  car, get onto the roof.
- **In a building:** go to the highest level. **Don't climb into a closed
  attic**, where you can be trapped by rising water. Go onto the roof only if
  necessary, and signal for help.

## After a flood

- **Go home only when authorities say it is safe.**
- Avoid driving unless it's an emergency.
- **Stay out of floodwater.** It can be contaminated, hide sharp debris, and be
  **electrically charged** by downed or underground power lines.
- **Don't touch electrical equipment if it's wet or you're standing in water.**
  Turn off the electricity only if it's safe to do so.
- Watch for snakes and other animals in your home.
- Run generators **only outdoors**, away from windows (see
  [carbon-monoxide.md](carbon-monoxide.md)).
- **Cleanup:** wear heavy gloves, protective clothing and boots. Children
  shouldn't help. People with asthma, other lung conditions or weak immune
  systems should stay out of buildings with mold or water leaks.
- Drinking water may be unsafe; see
  [../03-water/making-water-safe.md](../03-water/making-water-safe.md).
