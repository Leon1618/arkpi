# Earthquake

## During the shaking: Drop, Cover, Hold On

1. **Drop** to your hands and knees.
2. **Cover** your head and neck with one arm and hand.
3. **Hold on** until the shaking stops.

**Where you are:**

- **Inside:** stay inside. **Don't run outside, and avoid doorways.**
- **In bed:** turn face down and cover your head and neck with a pillow.
- **In a car:** pull over, stop, and set the parking brake.
- **Outside:** stay outside. Move to an open area away from buildings, trees,
  streetlights and power lines.

## Right after

- **Expect aftershocks.** Drop, Cover and Hold On again each time.
- **Check yourself for injuries first**, then help others if you can (see
  [severe-bleeding.md](severe-bleeding.md), [cpr.md](cpr.md)).
- **If the building is damaged, get out** and stay well away from it. **Don't
  go into damaged buildings.**
- **Near the coast?** Go inland or to higher ground **as soon as the shaking
  stops**. A tsunami can follow.
- **Smell gas?** See [gas-leak.md](gas-leak.md).

## If you are trapped

- Send a text message, or **bang on a pipe or wall** so rescuers can hear you.
- Use a **whistle** instead of shouting.
- Cover your mouth with your shirt to filter dust.
