# Power Outage

## Right away

- **Keep the fridge and freezer closed.**
  - Fridge: keeps food cold for about **4 hours**.
  - Full freezer: keeps its temperature for about **48 hours**.
- **Unplug electronics and appliances.** Power can come back with surges that
  damage them.
- **Extreme heat or cold?** Go to a community location that has power (ask
  local officials where heating or cooling centers are open).

## Stay safe

> **Never heat your home with a gas stove or oven.** It risks carbon monoxide
> poisoning and fire. See [carbon-monoxide.md](carbon-monoxide.md).

**Generators:**

- **Only outdoors**, at least **20 feet (6 m)** from windows, doors and attached
  garages.
- Keep it dry. Touching a wet generator or anything plugged into it can shock
  you.
- Connect appliances with **heavy-duty extension cords**.
- **Let it cool before refueling.**
- Have working **CO alarms on every level** of your home.

## Food and medicine

- **Throw out food that has been at 40 °F (4 °C) or warmer for 2 hours or
  more**, or that smells, looks or feels unusual. **When in doubt, throw it
  out!** Use a thermometer if you have one.
- **Medicine that needs refrigeration:** if the power is out for **more than a
  day**, throw it out unless the label says otherwise, and contact your doctor
  or pharmacist for a new supply.

## Before it happens

- If you rely on powered medical devices or refrigerated medicine, ask your
  doctor now for a plan, including how long your medicine lasts at higher
  temperatures.
- Keep a thermometer in the fridge and freezer.
