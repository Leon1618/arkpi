# Tornado

Tornadoes do happen around Noosa: one hit **Kin Kin** in 1971, killing three
people (see [../12-noosa-tewantin/local-hazards.md](../12-noosa-tewantin/local-hazards.md)).

## Warning signs

- A **rotating, funnel-shaped cloud**
- An approaching **cloud of debris**
- A **loud roar, like a freight train**

## At home or in a building

1. **Go straight to the safest place you've already chosen**: a small,
   **interior, windowless room** on the **lowest floor** of a sturdy building.
2. **Keep away from windows, doors and outside walls.**
3. **Protect your head and neck** with your arms. Use furniture or blankets for
   extra cover.

If you can't stay at home, go to a public shelter.

## In a car

There's no safe option in a car, only less dangerous ones.

- If the tornado is far away, visible, and traffic is light, you may be able to
  drive away at **right angles** to its path.
- Caught by strong wind or flying debris: **park out of the traffic lanes, stay
  buckled in and keep your head below the windows.**
- If there's ground **clearly lower than the road** nearby, get out and lie down
  there.
- **Don't shelter under bridges.**
