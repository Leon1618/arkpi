# Sheltering in Place

Stay inside when going out is more dangerous: for example, when there's a lot of
**debris in the air**, or authorities say the **air is badly contaminated**
(smoke, chemicals). Listen to the radio for official advice:
**ABC Sunshine Coast 90.3 / 95.3 FM**.

## Right away

1. **Bring everyone and the pets inside.**
2. **Lock doors. Close windows, air vents and any fireplace damper.**
3. **Turn off fans, air-conditioning and heating** that draw in outside air.
4. Take your **emergency kit**, unless you think it's contaminated.
5. Go to an **inside room with few or no windows**.

## Sealing a room (chemicals or bad air)

A temporary barrier against outside air. Prepare the materials **before** you
need them: keep **thick plastic sheeting and duct tape** in your emergency kit.

1. Cut plastic sheets **several centimetres wider** than each window, door and
   vent, and label them.
2. **Tape the corners first, then all the edges.**
3. Fill any other gaps with whatever you have.

## Going to a public shelter

Evacuation centres may have food, water and basic toilets, but **bring your own
emergency kit**. Most public shelters don't take pets, so plan somewhere for
them in advance.
