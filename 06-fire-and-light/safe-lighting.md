# Safe Lighting in a Power Cut

> **Use torches, not candles.** Candles cause house fires during power outages.

## Before the outage

- [ ] A torch (flashlight) for each person, kept where you can find it in the dark
- [ ] Plenty of **spare batteries**
- [ ] A **backup battery in your smoke alarm**

## If you must use a candle

- Put it in a **sturdy holder on a flat surface**.
- Keep it **at least 30 cm (12 inches) from anything that can burn**: curtains,
  paper, bedding.
- **Keep children and pets away** from it.
- **Blow it out** when you leave the room or go to sleep.

## Don't

- Don't use a gas stove, oven or barbecue for light or heat indoors (see
  [../01-emergency/carbon-monoxide.md](../01-emergency/carbon-monoxide.md)).
