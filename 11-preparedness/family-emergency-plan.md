# Family Emergency Plan

Make the plan **together**, write it down, and keep a copy in your emergency kit.
**Review it at least once a year** (Noosa Council suggests a weekend in
**October**, before the November–April disaster season) and whenever you move.

> Write your family's real details (names, phone numbers, addresses, medical
> information) in your own copy, kept in the `private/` folder or on paper.
> **Not** in this shared ark.

## Decide now

- [ ] **What could happen here?** (Noosa: cyclone, flood, storm tide, bushfire,
      heatwave; see [../12-noosa-tewantin/local-hazards.md](../12-noosa-tewantin/local-hazards.md))
- [ ] **Stay or go?** Decide in advance for each hazard whether you'll shelter at
      home or leave.
- [ ] **Where will you go if you leave?** Ask family or friends outside the risk
      area now whether you can stay with them.
- [ ] **How will you get there?** Your evacuation route, and a second one.
- [ ] **Where will you meet** if you're apart: one place near home, one outside
      the area.
- [ ] **What if it happens while you're at work or school?** Who picks up the
      children? Who checks on anyone who needs help?
- [ ] **How and when to call 000 or 112.** Make sure children know.
- [ ] **How to turn off water, power and gas** at the mains.
- [ ] **Pets:** where they'll go (most shelters don't take them) and 3 days of
      their food.

## Have ready

- [ ] **Emergency kit** for at least **3 days** at home (more if you could be cut
      off): see [emergency-kit.md](emergency-kit.md)
- [ ] **Evacuation kit** to take with it: sleeping bag, warm clothes, blankets
      and pillows, valuables, and things for children
- [ ] **Important documents** and some **cash** in sealed waterproof bags
- [ ] Medicines and prescriptions (see [../02-first-aid/medicines-in-an-emergency.md](../02-first-aid/medicines-in-an-emergency.md))
- [ ] A **full fuel tank** when bad weather is forecast
- [ ] **Noosa ALERT** set up for warnings by SMS and email
- [ ] Your **insurance** checked, and enough to cover you

## Check on others

Know your neighbours. When a warning comes, check on anyone who may need help:
older people, people living alone, people with a disability.

Get Ready Queensland has an online planner that makes a printable plan:
https://plan.getready.qld.gov.au
