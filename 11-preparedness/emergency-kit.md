# Emergency Supply Kit

A kit you can use at home or take with you. Check it every six months and
replace expired food, water, medicine and batteries.

## Basic kit

- [ ] **Water**: one gallon (≈3.8 L) per person per day, for several days, for
      drinking and sanitation
- [ ] **Food**: a several-day supply of non-perishable food
- [ ] Battery-powered or hand-crank radio, and a NOAA Weather Radio with tone
      alert (US)
- [ ] Flashlight
- [ ] First aid kit
- [ ] Extra batteries
- [ ] Whistle to signal for help
- [ ] Dust mask to filter contaminated air
- [ ] Plastic sheeting, scissors and duct tape to shelter in place
- [ ] Moist towelettes, garbage bags and plastic ties for personal sanitation
- [ ] Wrench or pliers to turn off utilities
- [ ] Manual can opener
- [ ] Local maps (paper)
- [ ] Cell phone with chargers and a backup battery

## Add based on your needs

- [ ] Soap, hand sanitizer and disinfecting wipes
- [ ] Prescription medications
- [ ] Non-prescription medications: pain relievers, anti-diarrhea medication,
      antacids, laxatives
- [ ] Prescription glasses and contact lens solution
- [ ] Infant formula, bottles, diapers, wipes, diaper rash cream
- [ ] Pet food and extra water for pets
- [ ] Cash
- [ ] Important family documents, saved electronically or in a waterproof
      container
- [ ] Sleeping bag or warm blanket for each person
- [ ] A complete change of clothes suited to your climate, and sturdy shoes
- [ ] Fire extinguisher
- [ ] Matches in a waterproof container
- [ ] Feminine supplies and personal hygiene items
- [ ] Mess kits, paper cups, plates, paper towels, plastic utensils
- [ ] Paper and pencil
- [ ] Books, games, puzzles and other activities for children

## Also keep

- [ ] A copy of this Arkpi folder on a USB drive
- [ ] A tourniquet (see [../01-emergency/severe-bleeding.md](../01-emergency/severe-bleeding.md))
